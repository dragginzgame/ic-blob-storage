## Measurement contract

Published Metrics 0.8.0 removes raw record_sample. The private storage probe now
uses MeasurementAggregate for completed wrapped reads, including measured zero.
Its sample count and instruction total are projected with their actual-overflow
flags; byte observations retain a separate u64 unit and sticky overflow flag.
Reaching a numeric cap exactly does not mark it clipped. Instruction totals remain
u128 in Candid and decimal strings in retained JSON. Both restoration and credit
reports project all three quality flags through the same report helper.

The host still owns the call-context counter, operator authority, exact wrapped
read attribution and begin/finish window. Every fresh window starts empty,
including all quality flags. No provenance is inferred from earlier clipped raw
fields. These are heap-only private observations, not production accounting;
rebuild the private probe and harness together. Old reports remain historical
inputs with their original format. No dual protocol, stored-data reset, new
production metrics or provider effect is introduced. Metrics' optional stable V1
encoding remains unchanged by the upstream cut.
