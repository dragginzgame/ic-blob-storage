# Caffeine provider contract and evidence

Blob's current operating contract uses [trusted registration](trusted-registration.md),
[bounded gateway callbacks](gateway-gc-contract.md) and
[provider-acknowledged funding](funding-credit.md). Certificate issuance records
retention intent and the first live reference. It does not establish uploaded
bytes, future availability, physical deletion or billing cessation.

The completion verifier, attestation endpoints and worker were retired in 0.41.
A matching successful Cashier callback permits a later distinct bounded payment;
independent credit proof is not required after every acknowledged payment.
Uncertain accepted effects remain blocked and their original identities survive.

The [service gap register](service-gaps.md) owns outstanding acceptance:
application publication/serving and multi-user journeys, authenticated payer
linkage, competing account activity, deployed deletion and billing settlement.
The [probe ledger](evidence/caffeine-probes/README.md) retains intent, budgets,
requests, results, hashes, failures and cleanup. Source reviews, substitutes and
live observations qualify only their recorded scope; they are not interchangeable.
This guide authorizes no paid effect or new provider probe.
