# Reporting ownership

Canic owns operational instrumentation, sampling and reporting through its shared
metrics adapter. Its Blob adapter projects the service's authoritative accounting
and records operations in Canic's provider registry. The integrating application
selects reporting and persistence policy in Canic; Blob adds no framework dependency,
second sampler, heap recorder roster, report DTOs or telemetry endpoint.

Blob owns storage accounting, quotas, references, funding journals, provider
liabilities and restoration fences. Existing service status, upload history,
reference receipts and operator inspection remain the authoritative interfaces.
Telemetry never substitutes for these records or proves provider deletion,
independent credit or billing cessation.

The private storage probe retains bounded resource measurements for local
qualification using local checked numeric totals. They have no Metrics dependency
and are excluded from standalone
deployment, rather than an application reporting service.

Historical provider observations retain their original released tools, packets,
artifacts and limitations. Removing a current reporting interface does not reset
an installed service or discard unfinished provider obligations.
